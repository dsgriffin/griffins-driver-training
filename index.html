Business Ethics

I have been a driving instructor for over 17 years and am a member of the Motor Schools Association.

I will structure your training to suit you as an individual and provide enjoyable driving lessons in a relaxed and friendly atmosphere.

I will not only teach you how to pass your driving test, but will coach you to obtain the skills and knowledge required to deal with today's road traffic conditions so that you will be both an efficient and safe driver.

Vehicle Used

The vehicle I use for driving lessons is a SEAT SE Tech TSI. It is a very popular car to learn to drive in, is simple to manoeuvre and is easy to get used to.
